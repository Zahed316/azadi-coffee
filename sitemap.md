# Sitemap

## Public Routes

| Page | Route | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Home | `/` | Brand, featured coffees, roastery story, latest posts |
| Shop | `/shop` | Product listing, filters, sorting |
| Product detail | `/shop/[slug]` | Coffee details and purchase |
| Cart | `/cart` | Review cart |
| Checkout | `/checkout` | Customer info, shipping, payment |
| Order confirmation | `/order/[id]` | Payment result and order summary |
| Blog | `/blog` | Articles and roastery notes |
| Blog post | `/blog/[slug]` | Article detail |
| About | `/about` | Roastery story and approach |
| Contact | `/contact` | Address, phone, form, social links |
| Wholesale | `/wholesale` | B2B supply inquiry |
